Portfolio Tracker

Add your holdings

Your quantities and costs stay in this browser. They are never uploaded to GitHub.

  1. Click Import holdings and choose your broker holdings file (.xlsx with an Equity sheet), or a CSV with the columns symbol, qty, avg_price, sector (add client for several portfolios, and use sector ETF for ETFs).
  2. Prices, market-cap groups and news fill in from the data files this site refreshes each weekday.
